# Amazon PA-API FAQ

## Do I need an Amazon Associates account?

**Yes, you need an Amazon Associates account to use PA-API.** However:

- ✅ You don't need to actively promote other products
- ✅ You can use it just for your own products
- ✅ You can earn affiliate commissions on your own products (this is allowed!)
- ⚠️ You must meet Amazon's sales requirements (see below)

## Can I use PA-API for my own products?

**Yes!** Amazon allows you to:
- Use PA-API to sync your own product listings
- Earn affiliate commissions on your own products
- Use your Associate Tag on your own product links

## What are the sales requirements?

To get and maintain PA-API access:

1. **Initial requirement**: Generate at least **3 qualified sales** through your Associate links
   - These can be sales of your own products
   - Once you have 3 sales, you can request PA-API access

2. **Ongoing requirement**: Generate at least **1 qualified sale per month**
   - If you don't meet this, Amazon may revoke your PA-API access
   - This is usually easy to maintain if you're driving traffic to your products

## What if I don't want to use Associates?

If you don't want to set up an Associates account, you have two options:

### Option 1: Manual Management (No API)
- Manage all product data manually in `data/brandCatalog.seed.json`
- Add prices, ratings, reviews, images manually
- No automatic syncing, but full control

### Option 2: Alternative Solutions
- Use web scraping (not recommended - violates Amazon's ToS)
- Use third-party services (may have costs)
- Build a manual update process

## Can I use PA-API without earning commissions?

**Technically yes, but practically no:**
- You still need the Associates account (which is designed for commissions)
- You can set your Associate Tag to not track commissions, but you still need the account
- Amazon requires sales activity to maintain access

## Best Practice for Your Own Products

Since these are your own products:

1. **Set up Associates account** - It's free and easy
2. **Use your Associate Tag** - You'll earn commissions on your own sales (bonus!)
3. **Drive traffic to your products** - This helps maintain the 1 sale/month requirement
4. **Enjoy automatic syncing** - Your products stay up-to-date automatically

## Summary

- ✅ **Need Associates account**: Yes
- ✅ **Can use for own products**: Yes
- ✅ **Can earn commissions on own products**: Yes (allowed by Amazon)
- ⚠️ **Must meet sales requirements**: Yes (3 initial, 1/month ongoing)

The Associates account is essentially your "key" to access PA-API. Even if you're not actively doing affiliate marketing, having the account allows you to use the API for your own products.
